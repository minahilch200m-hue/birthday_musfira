For Musfira,

Twenty years. And here you are — still standing, still trying, still you.

I know this season feels strange. The path feels foggy, the years blur together, and sometimes it seems like everyone else has already figured something out that we have not. But Musfira — that is not falling behind. That is being human.

We chose a hard road. Late nights staring at code that refuses to compile, deadlines that sneak up like they have a grudge, and the quiet weight of wondering if it will ever click. It will. It already is.

You are not a draft. You are not a rough sketch waiting to be finished. You are already someone worth knowing — curious, thoughtful, and braver than you give yourself credit for.

Uncertain paths are not wrong paths. Sometimes they lead somewhere the map never knew to show.

I am proud of you — not for the grades or the output, but for showing up. Every single time. I am here, and I am not going anywhere.

Happy 20th, my dear friend. 🌷

Duas for you

A few duas, just for you

رَّبِّ زِدْنِي عِلْمًا

"My Lord, increase me in knowledge."

Surah Ta-Ha, 20:114

رَبَّنَا آتِنَا فِي الدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً وَفِي الْآخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً وَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ

"Our Lord, grant us good in this world and good in the Hereafter, and protect us from the punishment of the Fire."

Surah Al-Baqarah, 2:201

رَبِّ إِنِّي لِمَا أَنزَلْتَ إِلَيَّ مِنْ خَيْرٍ فَقِيرٌ

"My Lord, I am truly in need of whatever good You may send down to me."

Surah Al-Qasas, 28:24

حَسْبُنَا اللَّهُ وَنِعْمَ الْوَكِيلُ

"Allah is sufficient for us, and He is the best disposer of affairs."

Surah Aal-Imran, 3:173

رَبِّ اشْرَحْ لِي صَدْرِي وَيَسِّرْ لِي أَمْرِي

"My Lord, expand my chest and ease my affairs for me."

Surah Ta-Ha, 20:25–26
